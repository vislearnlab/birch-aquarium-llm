Octopuses and their cousins first evolved nearly 470 million years ago. That's 50 million years older than sharks, 100 million years older than trees, and more than 200 million years older than dinosaurs!

Octopuses have 9 brains: one central brain, then 8 smaller brains to help control each arm.

Giant Pacific octopuses are the largest octopus species. They can weigh 200 pounds and have an arm span that can reach up to 30 feet.

Giant Pacific octopuses have 200 suction cups on each arm. Each suction cup has more taste receptors than the human tongue.

Giant Pacific octopuses can change both the color and the texture of their skin almost instantly — they are masters of disguise.

Octopuses are very intelligent and require daily enrichment and play. A Giant Pacific octopus can recognize its caretakers by sight and smell, knows how to solve puzzles, open jars, and enjoys interacting with guests.
