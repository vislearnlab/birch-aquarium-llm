# Sea Urchins — birch-ask-v2 video captions

source: video-caption:sea_urchins

Sea urchins are related to sea stars. Their bodies are divided into 5 equal sections.

Sea urchins have suction-cup-like tube feet they use to move around. Their favorite food is algae.

There are more than 200 different types of sea urchins found all over the world.

Sea urchins can move each one of their spines independently.

Sea urchins use their spines for protection, but some animals have tough teeth and jaws and can still break into them.

Sea urchins have 5 teeth that grow through their whole lives. Their special jaw structure is called Aristotle's Lantern.

These unique teeth inspired engineers and marine biologists at UC San Diego's Jacobs School of Engineering to create a claw-like device that can scoop up material in extraterrestrial environments — it might be used on a lunar or Mars rover one day.
