Named Loggerhead because of their huge heads and strong jaws.

These huge jaws allow them to eat a variety of food. They can crunch large snails and bivalves, catch slippery fish, or slurp down soft jellies and even seaweed.

Loggerheads are the second largest type of sea turtle and the longest hard-shelled sea turtle.

They can grow to weigh more than 250 pounds.

A Loggerhead Sea Turtle can be target trained, which means it knows to swim to a red target to receive a food reward.

Loggerhead females return to the same beach where they were born to lay their eggs.

They are the most common sea turtle found in US waters, and are also found worldwide in tropical and temperate seas.

All seven species of sea turtle are listed as threatened or endangered.

Impacts from pollution, loss of nesting beaches, climate change, and accidental and intentional capture are challenges for all sea turtles.
